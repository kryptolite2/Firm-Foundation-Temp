Ministries

To fulfill our mission and vision, we offer various ministries that cater to all age groups and spiritual needs

Sunday Services @8am

Join us in church and draw closer to Jesus—where hearts are healed, faith is strengthened, and hope is renewed

Kids Church (6 yrs - 12 yrs)

A fun and faith-filled space where children learn about God, build friendships, and grow in His love

NexGen Youth (+13 yrs)

A passionate community of young believers growing in faith, building friendships, and making an impact for Christ

Gather with us every Tuesday at 7pm for a time of prayer, worship, and encouragement. It’s a moment to seek God’s presence, lift up our needs, and grow in faith together
