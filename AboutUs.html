Welcome to Firm Foundation Church, where we are passionate about building lives on the unshakable foundation of God's Word.

At Firm Foundation Church, our mission is simple: to love God, love people, and make disciples. We believe that Jesus Christ is the firm foundation upon which we stand, and we strive to live out His teachings in everything we do. Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or looking for a community to grow deeper in your walk with God, we invite you to join us on this journey.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a vibrant community of believers who are rooted in faith, committed to personal growth, and actively sharing the love of Christ with the world. We envision a church where individuals and families thrive in their relationship with God, and where the message of hope, love, and grace spreads beyond our walls into our neighborhoods and beyond.

Our Beliefs

Firm Foundation Church stands firm on the authority of the Bible as the inspired Word of God. We believe that salvation is through faith in Jesus Christ alone, and that His death, burial, and resurrection offer forgiveness of sins and eternal life. We are committed to living out the teachings of Jesus through worship, service, and loving one another.

Our Values

Firm Foundation Church stands firm on the authority of the Bible as the inspired Word of God. We believe that salvation is through faith in Jesus Christ alone, and that His death, burial, and resurrection offer forgiveness of sins and eternal life. We are committed to living out the teachings of Jesus through worship, service, and loving one another.

Our core values are:

  • Truth – We are committed to teaching and living by the Word of God, which serves as our ultimate guide.
  • Prayer – We believe in the power of prayer to transform lives, strengthen faith, and align us with God’s will.
  • Worship – Our worship is a heartfelt response to God's love and grace, expressed through praise, prayer, and devotion.
  • Community – We foster a loving and supportive environment where believers can grow together in faith and unity.
  • Service – We strive to serve our church, our community, and the world with Christ-like love and compassion.
  • Discipleship – We are committed to equipping and mentoring believers to deepen their faith and live out their calling.
  • Outreach & Evangelism – We actively share the Gospel and extend God’s love beyond our church walls to impact lives.
